# History of the working notes

Detail moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 14 October 2026 so that each session reads less. Nothing here is needed to start work; read it when the detail of an experiment or of a specification version matters. The specification's own section 16 remains the record of every decision.

- **Experiment 1:** done. Correctness passed bitwise on scalar, AVX2 and AVX-512 here, and on NEON in CI, and its check passes in CI on Linux x86, Linux ARM, Windows and macOS on ARM. The arithmetic runs at memory speed; the whole pass does not, because the prototype makes whole planes in fresh memory.

- **Experiment 1b:** done (`RESULTS-1b.md`). `src/rolling.rs`: `Pass::new` plans a read once, `Pass::run(img, isa, ReadOptions, out)` fills the caller's Slices with nothing allocated per read; a ring of three rows a Level (five for `binomial5`); `halve_row` reads even and odd columns with vector shuffles. Same bits as experiment 1 on every test and every Window of 25 small Images; 3 to 5 times faster on the fastest instruction set. `pixel.raw` 1.7 times a warm copy at Level 0; `complex.polar` and `color.hsv` 1.4 to 1.7 times their own arithmetic at Level 0. **Missing data** (a validity mask on `Image`): one weight row a Level shared by every plane; `halve_weighted`, `halve_row_weighted`, `halve_over`; `missing_weights` `Equal` (default) or `PixelCount` (version 67); `missing_value` applied last; bitwise against `testing::masked_reference`, a float64 check of the rule, and `PixelCount` = pyramid(data) / pyramid(mask). 1.2 to 1.8 times an unmasked read. **Other kinds** (`Func::reduce`): `class.labels` keeps every second value, `depth.sensor.range` takes the least present value of each 3 × 3 block (`min3_present`, `min3_row`), `pixel.mask` gives `valid` and `valid_fraction` from the averaged mask; `LevelMethod::Decimate` keeps for every kind. Each checked against its definition. **`binomial5`:** `LevelMethod::Binomial5`, a ring of five rows a Level and margins of radius 2, planned with `Pass::for_method`; kernels `blur5`, `blur5_row`, `blur5_weighted`, `blur5_row_weighted`, `blur5_over`, and `row_taps` for three or five taps; bitwise against `testing::reference` (any filtering method, with or without a mask), float64 to 1.9e-7, a quarter of the amplitude at the halved grid's highest frequency; 0.9 to 1.3 times `bilinear` over two runs. **Threads:** `BandedPass::new(img, func, window, top, method, threads)` splits a read into bands of rows starting on multiples of 2^top, each its own `Pass`, writing into its rows through stack-held views (`Views`, `run_views`); bitwise equal to one thread, checked into NaN-filled Slices with the bands used reported; 1.65 to 2.1 times on two threads over three runs (table in `RESULTS-1b.md`); the threads are a `Pool` (`src/pool.rs`) started at planning, which spins up to 50 µs before sleeping (`Pool::run(&mut self, ...)`, panics raised after every part ends), so a read allocates nothing, as exp1b's counting global allocator checks; `Isa::is_available` replaces the allocating `available().contains`; `band_threads(window, threads)` gives one thread for each `MIN_BAND_PIXELS` (32,768) pixels, since waking costs tens of µs. **Coarse Levels alone:** `Pass::for_levels(img, func, window, first, top, method)`; where values are kept (`decimate`, labels: `LevelMethod::taps` is 1) the first Level is read from the Image at its grid points by `convert_row_step` and nothing else is read (`Pass::pixels_read`); Level 4 alone reads 1 in 256 pixels, 0.017 times a copy; averaged methods still read everything; a Window with no grid point asked for reads nothing. Proposals accepted by the author in version 70: one 2D average rather than rescaling after each pass, stored NaN counted as missing (tested: identical to masking the pixel), `valid_fraction` weighted by the filter, and `valid` following the averaged mask beside kinds that are not averaged, and labels at coarse Levels alone read at their grid points only under any method. Its check runs in CI.

- **Experiment 5:** done (`experiments/exp5-borrowed-arrays/RESULTS.md`). `bil_exp5`, a Python extension module against the stable interface (3.11 on), declared by hand in `src/ffi.rs` since PyO3 cannot be fetched here; `src/map.rs` maps with `mmap` or `MapViewOfFile` (`Shared`, `Private`) or reads (`Off`); Fragments and Slices export the buffer protocol and hold the memory through an `Arc`; `close()` frees the Source while borrowed arrays keep only the memory; reads release the interpreter lock; `build.rs` links (macOS `dynamic_lookup`, Windows `python3.lib`, MSVC only). `check.py` (needs NumPy): valid after the Source goes, under 8 threads, read-only under `Shared`, contiguity requests, alignment copies, nothing leaked; raises `::error::` annotations in CI. Passes on all four systems, 3.12, and 3.11 on Linux. Found for the author, all accepted in version 70: M3 (close), M13 (`Private` writes shared, data race the caller's), M14 (section 12 rule 9 against section 4), M15 (PyO3 or own declarations).

- **Experiment 3:** done for libjpeg-turbo 2.1.5 and OpenJPEG 2.5.0 (`experiments/exp3-c-errors/RESULTS.md`), and for GDAL 3.8.4 on 14 October 2026 (below). A workspace of its own (root `exclude`), since it links system libraries: `c/shim.c` (libjpeg `error_exit` longjmps to the `setjmp` in the C function that called the library; warnings counted; 500-scan progress monitor; `max_memory_to_use`; header and decoded sizes checked against limits; OpenJPEG callbacks, memory streams, `OPJ_DPARAMETERS_IGNORE_PCLR_CMAP_CDEF_FLAG`), `c/state.c` (locale, signals with masks, blocked mask, FP modes without flags, umask, cwd, environment, fds; FP flags apart; `mallinfo2`), `src/main.rs` (supervisor and workers, seeded mutations, workers under `GLIBC_TUNABLES` with tcache and fast bins off so heap counts are exact, and `perturb` pairs), `vendor/` headers (the workspace has runtimes only; written `jconfig.h`, `opj_config.h`, `opj_stdint.h`; OpenJPEG version asserted at start). 5.2 million corrupt files: no crash, exit, hang, output, leak or state change. CI job `c-errors` on ubuntu-24.04 and ubuntu-24.04-arm. Found for the author, all accepted in version 70: M16 (libjpeg warnings as corrupt data), M17 (a scan limit, a working-memory cap, refusals as errors), M18 (JP2 palettes as indices; `cmap` and `cdef` the driver's).

- **Experiment 3, GDAL's part** (14 October 2026): `c/gdal.c`, built only with the `gdal` feature through `gdal-config`, `c/gdal_stub.c` elsewhere, `c/layer.h` shared with `shim.c`. `--gdal` runs NITF and TIFF (`Codec::Nitf`, `Codec::Tiff`), seeds made by GDAL (NITF NC, C3 at quality 90, blocked NC, C3 at quality 75; TIFF none, deflate tiled, LZW band, JPEG tiled), two digit mutations for NITF only so the other formats' files are unchanged. Layer: `CPLPushErrorHandlerEx`, `CPLErrorSetState` to restore the last error, thread-local `GDAL_DISABLE_READDIR_ON_OPEN` and `GDAL_PAM_ENABLED` put back after, `GDALRegister_*` one by one, `GDALOpenEx` with one allowed driver and `GDAL_OF_VERBOSE_ERROR`, a fixed `/vsimem/` name so messages repeat. GDAL state: `CPLGetErrorHandler` (3.7), last error, global and thread options, drivers in order, open datasets, `/vsimem/` listing, cache, `dl_iterate_phdr` count; seven pokes. Found: GDAL's GeoTIFF driver returns warned JPEG tiles from an unwritten block buffer (TIFF 2,861, valgrind: `GDALRasterBlock::Internalize`), so the pair check compares delivered outcomes (`Outcome::delivered`) for every codec; TIFF 1,197,389 declares a 16 × 32,784 tile of 14,974 bands, 7.85 GB, killed by the OOM killer, so the layer refuses a block over twice `max_decoded_bytes` (`GDALGetBlockSize`). Full run 400,000 NITF and 4,000,000 TIFF pass. Built and run here against GDAL 3.8.4 built from source with PROJ 9.4.0 (a `sqlite3` stand-in for PROJ's build) in the session's scratch space, which does not last; CI job `gdal-errors` uses Ubuntu's libgdal-dev. Proposals M22 (cap where no cap; counts every band) and M23 (registering in a shared GDAL).

- **NITF container reader** (14 October 2026): `crates/bil/src/nitf/` (`mod.rs` reader, `defs.rs` layouts and walk), `fields.rs`, `types.rs`, `io.rs` (`ReadAt` for files and bytes). Layouts are ordered items with fixed or field-given lengths, presence conditions and counted runs (M21, accepted in version 73); fields in runs always numbered `[n]`, nested `[n][m]` (M26). Opening reads the file header and image subheaders only, from 512 bytes up as the walk needs; extensions in the headers are found at open, TRE_OVERFLOW segments read on first request for their header's groups (`OnceLock`), groups extracted on request and held in a `Mutex<HashMap>`. RPC00A/B defined; others as `CEDATA`. A defined field the file leaves out is `Missing`, with as many repeat numbers as its runs; anything else unknown is `NotFound`. A damaged extension area makes the header group `Partial` (M27). Masks: BMR offsets in any order, `0xFFFFFFFF` missing, TPXCD pad when every sample is pad (M24). Overflow guarded by a checked block-size product at open and a checked Window size, after the checking agent found a header that overflowed. Tests: `crates/bil/tests/nitf.rs`, a writer independent of the reader, 20,000 corruptions per CI run. `examples/nitf_dump.rs` against `testdata/nitf_reference.c` through `testdata/compare_nitf.py`; CI's `test data` job compares the 22 manifest files and GDAL 3.8.4's own 19: all agree (blocks never recorded are where GDAL writes its pad value, so only recorded pixels are compared; `v_3301f.ntf` leaves out 12 of 16 blocks). The million-file run and the 17-of-19 local comparison are in `crates/bil/tests/RESULTS-nitf.md`.
